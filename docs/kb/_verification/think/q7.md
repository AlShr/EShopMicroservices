# What is published to RabbitMQ, and by whom?

## Answer

A `BasketCheckoutEvent` is published to RabbitMQ by the **Basket API**, specifically from its `CheckoutBasketHandler` during checkout. The handler maps `BasketCheckoutDto` to `BasketCheckoutEvent`, sets the event’s `TotalPrice` from the stored basket, and identifies the next step as sending that event through MassTransit to RabbitMQ.
